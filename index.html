Good to Know

Frequently asked

✿
Where do you travel?

We serve the entire San Francisco Bay Area — San Francisco, Marin, the Peninsula, Silicon Valley, and the East Bay. A modest travel fee may apply beyond 20 miles; we’ll always confirm it before you commit.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes. We highly recommend a trial for weddings so we can perfect your look in advance. Add a note when you book and we’ll arrange a session a few weeks before your date.

Can you do my whole bridal party?

Absolutely. Select the number of people when booking and tell us your timeline — for larger parties we bring additional artists so everyone is ready on schedule.

What does it cost?

Services start at the prices listed, and the final quote depends on your service, party size, and location. You’ll receive a clear, all-in quote when we confirm — no surprises.

How far in advance should I book?

Weddings and peak-season dates book months ahead. For everyday appointments, a few days’ notice is usually plenty — but the calendar shows real availability, so grab your slot while it’s open.

What about hygiene?

Every kit is fully sanitized between clients, brushes are cleaned and disinfected, and disposables are used wherever appropriate. Your skin’s health comes first.
